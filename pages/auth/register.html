Terms of Use & Privacy

Demo A plain-language summary for this student project.

Using TutorAI

  • Upload only lecture material you’re allowed to use for your own study.
  • AI answers come from your lecture text but can still be wrong — check important points against your course.
  • Don’t use TutorAI to complete graded work where your course doesn’t allow AI help.

Your data

  • We store your profile, courses, lecture text, quiz results and AI Tutor chats so the app can work.
  • Passwords are stored only as secure hashes on the server, never in your browser.
  • Lecture text is sent to the AI service only to answer your questions; it isn’t shared with other students.
  • You can download all your data or delete your account at any time in Settings.

In this demo nothing leaves your browser: data is kept in a local sample store.
